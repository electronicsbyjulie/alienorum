HORIZON PERFORMANCE TRIALS

Protocol
========
The item numbers follow the twelve findings in the local draw_horizon audit.
Each trial compares its original path with the candidate on the same executable,
scenes, poses, animation times, source pixels, and movement distances. All
previously retained items remain active in both arms. Alternating
order reduces thermal and cache-order bias. A separate source generation starts
each arm, followed by two stationary settling frames and three movement warmup
frames. Moving views use the same
base-color sampling quality in both arms. End and Shift+End use their current
control calculation at a fixed seven-frame-per-second timestep.

Metrics cover find_horizon plus draw_horizon preparation, thread CPU time on
Linux, and completion of the submitted horizon draw through glFinish. Pixel
readback occurs outside timed samples. These are horizon-stage measurements,
not whole-application FPS estimates. Current trials use hidden windows on the
desktop graphics context; the log records the GL renderer. Software OpenGL
results are diagnostic only. The paired driver stops each child at 90 seconds,
with a two-second hard-stop grace period and an outer process-group watchdog.

Worlds: generated Tau-Ceti-like ice, authored Earth coastal maps, a dense ocean,
forest, Venus-like hot haze, an airless rock, and a lava world. Modes: stationary,
continuous panning, normal walking, and tenfold walking. Eight alternating paired
rounds confirm retained candidates; borderline regressions receive further checks.
Median and p95 completed-frame results both matter. Preparation and CPU results
provide supporting evidence. For trials 203 onward, retention calls for at least
a 15 percent reduction in median completed frame time in the target scene,
with controls checked for regressions.
Image and live-invalidation tests are required as well. No gain is inferred from
timing noise, and a faster stationary view does not excuse slower movement.

Driver example:
python3 tools/run_horizon_trials.py --trial 1 --rounds 8 --output /tmp/horizon-trial-01-native

---------------------------------------------------

bound uses the lowest skyline profile slope at a known distance in every

Follow-up to trials 203–205
---------------------------
At the end of trial 205 the coast took about a second per frame with software OpenGL.
Profile the exact mixed-tile scan against wave classification and draw
submission before selecting another optimization. A coastal material atlas
could certify uniformly wet and dry regions across multiple source pixels.
Water phase, basin level, biome displacement and uncertain boundaries must
participate in certification. Keep pixel checks for narrow coast crossings,
ice edges, shoreline continuity during walks and edits to the source map.

Plant layout and appearance currently share one scenery cache revision. Measure
plant-site selection and canopy clearance when walking across patch boundaries
and when the local surface phase publishes. A plant-site cache keyed to the
permanent terrain could preserve site selections through seasonal appearance
updates. The stationary projection failure points to the habitat cache: a map
generation update can replace its baseline using the edited radius or pressure.
Distinguish an unchanged map's revision from a new terrain source before trying
to retain site selections through these edits.

Separately measure depth uploads, projected shadows and GPU crown
shading with nearby volumetric crowns visibly active. Confirm any candidate
on hardware OpenGL in addition to the software path.

Trial 207: Retain plant-site density through clock updates
---------------------------------------------------------

established habitat. A per-site cache keeps that value while the renderer
refreshes current water coverage, frost, canopy clearance and crown appearance.
Map revisions, terrain inputs, species inputs and a new scenery patch refresh

forest view panned while the simulation clock advanced one hour per pose.
Both paths drew volumetric plants and every measured image matched exactly.

reduction with a paired interval of 15.13 to 18.23 percent. The p95 fell from
