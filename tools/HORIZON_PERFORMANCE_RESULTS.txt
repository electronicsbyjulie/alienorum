HORIZON PERFORMANCE TRIALS

Protocol
========
The item numbers follow the twelve findings in the local draw_horizon audit.
Each trial compares its original path with the candidate on the same executable,
scenes, poses, animation times, source pixels, and movement distances. All
previously retained items remain active in both arms. Alternating
order reduces thermal and cache-order bias. A separate source generation starts
each arm, followed by two stationary settling frames and three movement warmup
frames. Moving views use the same
base-color sampling quality in both arms. End and Shift+End use their current
control calculation at a fixed seven-frame-per-second timestep.

Metrics cover find_horizon plus draw_horizon preparation, thread CPU time on
Linux, and completion of the submitted horizon draw through glFinish. Pixel
readback occurs outside timed samples. These are horizon-stage measurements,
not whole-application FPS estimates. Current trials use hidden windows on the
desktop graphics context; the log records the GL renderer. Software OpenGL
results are diagnostic only. The paired driver stops each child at 90 seconds,
with a two-second hard-stop grace period and an outer process-group watchdog.

Worlds: generated Tau-Ceti-like ice, authored Earth coastal maps, a dense ocean,
forest, Venus-like hot haze, an airless rock, and a lava world. Modes: stationary,
continuous panning, normal walking, and tenfold walking. Eight alternating paired
rounds confirm retained candidates; borderline regressions receive further checks.
Median and p95 completed-frame results both matter. Preparation and CPU results
provide supporting evidence. A retained candidate must show an improvement and
no supported completed-frame regression, especially in the three moving modes.
Image and live-invalidation tests are required as well. No gain is inferred from
timing noise, and a faster stationary view does not excuse slower movement.

Driver example:
python3 tools/run_horizon_trials.py --trial 1 --rounds 8 --output /tmp/horizon-trial-01-native

Item 1: Retain projected terrain and the CPU depth raster
========================================================
Status: retained.

Implementation: retain the CPU projected terrain and depth raster under a mesh
epoch plus the full projection/raster settings. The mesh epoch advances when
world geometry changes. Walking and panning invalidate the view key; no travel
tolerance was introduced for terrain geometry. Disabling terrain invalidates the
retained raster. A developer reference control selects the uncached path.

Intel HD Graphics 2000, eight paired rounds, six timed frames per arm and mode:
Scene     Stationary before/after   Pan before/after   Walk before/after   10x before/after
Ice          27.72 / 22.19            35.99 / 36.15       34.23 / 34.60      38.33 / 38.64
Earth        32.78 / 29.11            67.46 / 67.84       45.20 / 44.95      78.84 / 78.36
Forest       18.08 / 13.70            27.75 / 27.85       26.32 / 25.84      28.62 / 29.36
Values are completed horizon-frame milliseconds, not app frame times. Paired
stationary changes were -20.15%, -10.56%, and -23.04%; their bootstrap intervals
excluded zero. Moving differences in these scenes were unresolved apart from a
faster forest walk. Ocean, hot haze, airless, and lava controls also ran.

The first matrix flagged lava walking at +2.47% (95% interval +0.35 to +7.13%).
A fresh twelve-pair check was unresolved: +3.37%, interval -3.20 to +6.99%.
The terrain-mesh path is inactive in that scene; no reproducible moving
regression was established. No other completed-frame median or p95 regression
was supported by the eight-pair matrix.

Quality: 42 focused image/behavior tests passed on the desktop GPU. Eight-round
paired image comparisons passed across all seven scenes and four motion modes.
The first harness attempt exposed a sampling-phase difference even in the
identical-path control. Two stationary settling frames now align the origin and
every measured frame asserts equal sampling stride. The existing one-level RGB
tolerance was not raised. The corrected identical-path control also passed.

Raw results: /tmp/horizon-trial-01-validated/ and
/tmp/horizon-trial-01-lava-confirm/. The initial software-GL pilot was excluded
from acceptance. The native graphics logs identify Mesa Intel HD Graphics 2000.

Item 2: Separate source painting from illumination
==================================================
Status: retained, with a conservative uniform-footprint scope.

The candidate proves that the entire possible visible ground footprint has one
source RGB/material value, including far terrain and biome displacement bounds.
It retains that source independently of live illumination and haze light. A
single cropped GPU quad uses current masks and the current byte-quantized color;
its coverage is refreshed even on a source-cache hit. Detailed or uncertain
footprints use the original painter. No new per-fragment shader work was added.

Eight paired rounds on Intel HD Graphics 2000, including a changing-light mode:
Scene/mode       Before ms   After ms   Paired change
Ocean/pan          23.56       19.44       -16.99%
Ocean/10x walk     19.27       15.68       -20.47%
Ocean/lighting     18.95       15.11       -21.31%
Forest/pan         27.78       24.27       -13.40%
Forest/10x walk    28.59       25.80        -9.48%
Forest/lighting    17.74       13.63       -23.79%
These intervals excluded zero. No completed-frame median or p95 regression was
supported in the seven-scene, five-mode matrix. Other scene/mode differences
were largely unresolved. The candidate is not a claim that every coast can
reuse its source painting across illumination edits.

Quality: 43 focused image/behavior tests passed, including live illumination and
redlight cache checks against the original painter. Paired per-frame sampling
stride and final image checks passed throughout the matrix.
Raw results: /tmp/horizon-trial-02-native/.

Item 3: Isolate map publication and cache suppression
====================================================
Status: rejected; production code and candidate-specific fixtures removed.

The tested candidate placed active writers in a bounded per-map registry.
Sampling checks performed no
allocation or locking. Nested writers retain ownership until publication; a full
hash bucket falls back conservatively to global suppression. File loaders,
procedural writers, and secondary opacity/facula maps participate. Completed bump
loads also advance the source generation, invalidating retained terrain geometry.
The existing general generation status remains available for other consumers.
This does not replace the application's texture ownership synchronization.

The seven-scene, five-mode native matrix passed image and stride checks. Eight
paired rounds also covered unrelated procedural generation and unrelated file
loading. For procedural-generation comparisons, the legacy path withholds valid
material tags and disables motion painting reuse. Equal stride or equal images
against that provisional path would preserve its incorrect global coupling.
Instead, the candidate's fully refined image is compared with quiescent rendering
at the same pose, source, and animation time, within the same one-level tolerance.
Ordinary and file-loading comparisons still assert matching stride and images.

Unrelated procedural generation, eight pairs on Intel HD Graphics 2000:
Scene     Still before/after   Pan before/after   Walk before/after   10x before/after
Ice        345.94 / 21.64       356.36 / 36.05     375.79 / 34.32     373.91 / 39.00
Earth       80.24 / 29.37        88.32 / 67.22     109.64 / 44.76     109.63 / 78.46
All completed-frame median intervals excluded zero in favor of the candidate.
Unrelated Earth file loading showed unresolved moving differences of +0.30%,
+0.44%, and -0.86% for pan, walk, and tenfold walking respectively.

Raw results: /tmp/horizon-trial-03-native/ and the corresponding
/tmp/horizon-trial-03-foreign_* directories. A fresh tail confirmation and
publication tests informed the evaluation. Forty-four focused rendering tests
passed. The unexecuted loader-version assertion was removed with the candidate.

Rejection: unrelated generation plus Earth tenfold walking improved the median
but worsened p95. Eight pairs showed +8.22%; a fresh twelve-pair confirmation
showed 110.99 -> 120.13 ms, +8.20%, interval +7.52 to +9.47%. This violates the
movement-tail criterion despite the large stationary and median gains. The
ordinary tenfold-walking tail flag did not repeat: twelve fresh pairs gave
-0.58%, interval -1.17 to +0.35%. No per-map registry is shipped by this trial.
Confirmation data: /tmp/horizon-trial-03-foreign-tail-confirm/ and
/tmp/horizon-trial-03-tail-confirm/. The driver retains a general mode selector
for focused repetitions; the temporary foreign-writer modes were removed.

Item 4: Retain GPU ground geometry
==================================
Status: rejected for lack of a convincing benefit; prototype removed.

The prototype retained expanded vertices in the existing four callback batches
and associated identical rectangle lists with one VBO revision. Context teardown
cleared all retained state. Masks and haze uniforms remained live. Eight paired
rounds across seven scenes and five modes passed image and sampling checks.
Stationary results were unresolved throughout: ice 21.62 -> 22.90 ms, Earth
29.77 -> 29.40 ms, and forest 13.22 -> 13.55 ms. Rectangle equality checks and
retained storage offered little advantage on these already cached painters.

Ocean tenfold walking initially flagged +7.57%; a fresh twelve-pair repetition
did not confirm it: median -2.79%, interval -4.32 to +3.20%; p95 -1.49%, interval
-7.56 to +4.43%. Ocean normal walking was faster in the first matrix (-3.83%),
but without a stationary gain or a consistent moving gain the additional cache
was not retained. This trial does not establish a reproducible regression.
Raw results: /tmp/horizon-trial-04-native/ and /tmp/horizon-trial-04-confirm/.

Item 5: Retain conservative depth bounds for tile certification
==============================================================
Status: rejected for insufficient benefit; prototype and dedicated test removed.

The prototype lazily built exact positive-depth min/max values for 16x16 blocks,
scanned partial edges directly, and invalidated with the retained terrain raster.
Forty-eight focused image/behavior tests passed, including direct bounds checks
at partial tiles, empty depth fields, invalidation, and changed dimensions.
Eight paired rounds covered seven scenes and five modes with matching images.

No panning gain was established: ice 37.26 -> 36.64 ms (paired +0.34%, unresolved),
Earth 66.82 -> 67.41 ms (+0.56%, unresolved). Earth changing-light frames gained
about 0.99%. Ordinary Earth walking initially flagged +0.59%, but a fresh twelve
pairs did not repeat it: -0.41%, interval -0.84 to +0.08%; p95 -0.64%, interval
-2.08 to -0.03%. The matrix also flagged forest lighting and Venus stationary
timings, where this depth-index path was inactive; those flags were not used as
evidence of a code-induced regression. Building and querying the index did not
produce a convincing gain in its target panning workload, so it was removed.
Raw results: /tmp/horizon-trial-05-native/ and /tmp/horizon-trial-05-confirm/.

Item 6: Streamline water setup and row geometry
==============================================
Status: retained.

A full-footprint RGB/material certificate can skip the entire water stage when
all possible lookups are dry. The radius includes the far terrain extent and
the sampler's biome displacement bound. Uncertain or mixed footprints retain
the original path. Row-constant rotation and wave amplitude are calculated once
per row during geometry construction; coast sampling and wave animation remain
unchanged. The runtime trial control retains the original calculations.

Eight paired rounds across seven scenes and five modes passed image/stride
comparisons. Forest panning improved 24.54 -> 20.75 ms (-15.63%); Earth panning
67.32 -> 66.34 ms (-1.91%); forest tenfold walking 26.32 -> 25.66 ms (-2.23%).
All three median intervals excluded zero. Other moving results were unresolved.

Initial p95 flags on Earth and ocean tenfold walking did not repeat in twelve
fresh pairs: Earth +0.05%, interval -1.07 to +1.81%; ocean +4.16%, interval -5.04
to +8.98%. The lava changing-light control also cleared its initial flag:
median +2.25%, interval -1.88 to +6.01%; p95 +0.83%, interval -6.35 to +10.58%.
No reproducible completed-frame regression was established.

Forty-three focused image/behavior tests passed on the final build. The dry-stage
assertion verifies skipped work against the original path's exact pixels.
Raw results: /tmp/horizon-trial-06-native/, /tmp/horizon-trial-06-confirm/, and
/tmp/horizon-trial-06-lava-confirm/.

Item 7: Translate retained terrain patches during short walks
============================================================
Status: rejected on image consistency; prototype removed.

The prototype retained world-fixed sampled rings over a short travel interval,
translated horizontal coordinates for the current observer, adjusted the height
datum, and rerasterized every changed pose. Source/physical changes rebuilt the
mesh. The first two-metre version improved the three-pair ice walking median
35.49 -> 29.14 ms (-16.10%) and Earth walking 44.78 -> 33.67 ms (-24.83%).
Ice final images differed at one or two pixels beyond the one-level RGB tolerance.

A quarter-metre interval passed final-pose checks at both six and seventeen timed
frames. Its six-frame pilot improved normal walking by 12.31% on ice and 22.38%
on Earth. Tenfold walking rebuilt the mesh and showed no established gain.
However, checking every replayed pose exposed a repeatable one-pixel difference
on ice at early normal-walking poses in all three rounds. This is a small image
change, not evidence of a conspicuous coastline shift, but the translated patch
does not exactly reproduce observer-centered sampling. It was removed rather
than relaxing the image criterion. No eight-pair acceptance claim is made.

The harness retains optional every-pose inspection, outside timed samples, via
ALIENORUM_HORIZON_INSPECT_EVERY_POSE=1. Raw logs:
/tmp/horizon-trial-07-pilot/, /tmp/horizon-trial-07-earth-pilot/,
/tmp/horizon-trial-07-tight-pilot/, /tmp/horizon-trial-07-long-pilot/, and
/tmp/horizon-trial-07-every-pose/.

Item 8: Reject zero-effect shadows and hoist per-light factors
=============================================================
Status: rejected for insufficient repeatable benefit; prototype removed.

The prototype rejected existing width/base failures before distance and haze
work, skipped zero-strength lights and exactly zero encoded shadow RGB, and
hoisted per-light direction trigonometry and length divisors. Zero RGB is an
identity operation for the multiplicative shadow blend. Thirty-two focused
image tests passed, and the seven-scene, five-mode eight-pair matrix matched
images and sampling strides.

Small initial gains included ice tenfold walking (-0.39%) and Earth tenfold
walking (-0.69%). Ice normal walking flagged +2.40%, while forest tenfold-walk
p95 flagged +3.33%. Twelve fresh ice pairs left both moving results unresolved:
walk +1.49%, interval -1.40 to +2.05%; tenfold -0.89%, interval -1.84 to +0.94%.
Their p95 changes were also unresolved. Twelve fresh forest pairs cleared the
tail flag: -0.41%, interval -3.32 to +1.77%. No reproducible regression was
established, but no convincing repeated gain justified retaining the logic.
Raw results: /tmp/horizon-trial-08-native/, /tmp/horizon-trial-08-confirm/, and
/tmp/horizon-trial-08-forest-confirm/.

Item 9: Retain projected rock facets and normals
===============================================
Status: rejected for insufficient repeatable benefit; prototype removed.

The prototype retained sorted facet positions and normals on a reused scenery
projection, while recomputing current illumination per facet. Moving views used
the original construction and cleared retained facets without filling the cache.
Twenty-nine focused rock/scenery/haze image tests passed. The seven-scene,
five-mode eight-pair matrix also matched images and sampling strides.

The intended stationary gain was small: airless 9.30 -> 9.23 ms (-0.85%). Earth
and ice stationary differences were unresolved. Forest stationary initially
flagged +5.88%; twelve fresh pairs left it unresolved (+1.96%, interval -2.82 to
+7.03%). Twelve fresh airless pairs also left the gain unresolved: 9.32 -> 9.20
ms, -1.53%, interval -2.03 to +1.35%; p95 -2.88%, interval -3.33 to +23.21%.
No repeatable regression was established, but the measured saving did not
justify the larger geometry cache and associated invalidation logic.
Raw results: /tmp/horizon-trial-09-native/ and /tmp/horizon-trial-09-confirm/.
