M-star display colors

Reference CIE chromaticities: Steven R. Cranmer (2021),
Brown Dwarfs are Violet: A New Calculation of Human-eye Colors of
Main-sequence Stars and Substellar Objects.
Paper: https://doi.org/10.3847/2515-5172/ac225c
Data: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.5293307
License: CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

PHOENIX solar-composition, log g=5 spectra between 2300 and 3900 K
supply a normalized visible-color reference. This reference palette is
also used for M giants for visual consistency; their different gravity
and individual molecular abundances are not separately modeled.
Mira optical-temperature changes retain their time-dependent hue.

Discs, glow, neighborhood swatches, and illumination share this palette.
Stored catalog photometry is preserved. Supplied temperature takes
priority, with subtype calibration as the fallback. Dwarf and subdwarf
prefixes are recognized. No extra saturation is applied to this palette.

Pretty Star Colors (View menu, Ctrl+J) selects the historical vivid
photometric palette for ordinary stars and adds 20 percent saturation
to white-dwarf display colors. Brown-dwarf colors stay unchanged.
Realistic mode uses the published reference spectra for ordinary stars,
including the hotter PHOENIX and hot-star reference entries in Cranmer's
table. Different reference gravities follow the published grid.
Generated textures retain a realistic baseline; a display tint switches
modes immediately. The preference is saved with user settings.
