Brown dwarf visible colors

CIE 1931 chromaticities from Steven R. Cranmer (2021),
"Brown Dwarfs are Violet: A New Calculation of Human-eye Colors of
Main-sequence Stars and Substellar Objects."
Paper: https://doi.org/10.3847/2515-5172/ac225c
Data: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.5293307

The compiled table uses solar-composition Sonora spectra (Marley et al.
2021) at log g = 5, from 200 to 2400 K. A 3000 K PHOENIX point
(Husser et al. 2013) supplies a display interpolation above 2400 K.
CIE xy is interpolated, converted to linear sRGB, brought into the display
gamut toward white, and normalized. This describes an amplified visible
hue, not infrared imagery or naked-eye detectability.

Cloud bands, patches, and vortices are procedural illustrations. Their
placement is repeatable for each object. Rotation, gravity, temperature,
and metallicity adjust the illustrative pattern; they do not constitute
an atmospheric circulation calculation or a resolved observational map.
Evidence for bands: Millar-Blanchaer et al. (2020),
https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-4357/ab6ef2
