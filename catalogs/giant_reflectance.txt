Cool giant reflection colors

Base spectra: Kerri Cahoy, Mark Marley, Jonathan Fortney (2010),
Exoplanet Albedo Spectra and Colors as a Function of Planet Phase,
Separation, and Metallicity. ApJ 724, 189.
https://doi.org/10.1088/0004-637X/724/1/189
https://roman.ipac.caltech.edu/page/exoplanet-characterization-html

The authors permit use of the spectra on condition that they receive credit.
This file and the catalog preserve that credit. The packaged grid uses their
full-phase models, linearly resampled to 5 nm from 350 to 1000 nm. It contains
Jupiter (1x, 3x solar) and Neptune (10x, 30x solar) atmospheres at 0.8, 2, 5,
and 10 AU around a solar analog. No paper PDF is part of the release payload.

Bessell B, V, R energy-response passbands: SVO Filter Profile Service,
Generic/Bessell.B, Generic/Bessell.V, Generic/Bessell.R (Bessell 1990).
https://svo2.cab.inta-csic.es/theory/fps/
Solar flux is from the spectrum distributed with the Cahoy grid. Broadband
indices use solar reference colors B-V=0.65 and V-R=0.36.

Display color integrates the same reflection spectrum with the CIE 1931
2-degree observer functions and converts XYZ to linear sRGB. The renderer
then applies its usual display gamma. B-V and V-R remain independent
photometric outputs; the display spectrum is not reconstructed from them.
CIE 2019, Colour-matching functions of CIE 1931 standard colorimetric observer,
International Commission on Illumination (CIE), Vienna, AT.
DOI: 10.25039/CIE.DS.xvudnb9b
https://cie.co.at/datatable/cie-1931-colour-matching-functions-2-degree-observer
CIE data license: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
The cmf_xyz columns are a 5 nm resampling of that dataset and retain this
license. The spectrum authors' credit condition also continues to apply.

Implementation assumptions, not observations:
- Interpolation in log separation/enrichment; interpolation between giant
  families by log mass. Outside-grid inputs use boundary spectra.
- A supplied temperature selects an equivalent solar separation. Otherwise
  stellar bolometric luminosity and orbit set irradiation, independently of
  the planet's estimated color. A fixed Bond 0.3 supplies the temperature proxy.
- Mass suggests envelope enrichment with broad, seeded scatter. Stellar
  metallicity is a weak prior; stellar carbon relative to iron modifies
  methane absorption weakly. Formation history remains unknown.
- Gravity and a seeded cloud scenario shift the cloud transition slightly.
- Bounded gray cloud mixing and blue-absorbing haze vary between eight bands.
  These extensions are illustrative approximations, not new atmosphere fits.
- Supplied methane, including zero, adjusts red absorption depths relative to
  a local continuum. This does not solve chemical equilibrium or line transfer.
- Other stellar spectra use a blackbody shape ratio to the reference solar
  spectrum. Stellar UV chemistry and detailed spectral lines are not modeled.
- Intrinsic heat and cloud microphysics are not reconstructed from mass alone.
- Colors represent full phase, not a phase-dependent spectral simulation.

The saved giant_color_model records estimates, assumptions, spectral indices,
and band colors. A supplied or edited B-V takes priority over this model.
The hot-giant and clear-skies color prescriptions are separate.
